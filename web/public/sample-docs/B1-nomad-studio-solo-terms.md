## Nature of the practice

The practice is a solo consulting practice owned solely by Marcus Tan. It is not a partnership, a joint venture, or a company with multiple members. All rights, obligations, and decisions rest with the sole owner, who is identified above.

Advisory services are provided in operations design, supplier selection, and commercial planning for small consumer brands. Engagements are accepted at the discretion of the sole owner. Work is performed personally unless the sole owner expressly authorises a subcontractor in writing.

This document sets out the standard basis on which services are offered. They apply to every engagement unless a signed statement of work expressly varies them. No other document should be read as creating a shared ownership arrangement or a second decision-maker.

## Fees and Billing

Professional services are billed at an hourly rate of $250. Time is recorded in increments and summarised in the monthly invoice. The fee covers preparation, calls, analysis, and written deliverables, and it is quoted before any work begins. The hourly rate covers analysis, preparation, meetings, and written deliverables.

Direct costs of $90 per engagement day are charged separately. Direct costs cover travel to the client site, local transport, and any third-party data or software required for that day's work. Direct costs are passed through at cost and are itemised on the invoice.

Invoices are issued at the end of each calendar month and are payable within fourteen days. Late payment may pause work until the account is settled. The sole owner may require an advance retainer before work begins on a new engagement.

## Margin and pricing discipline

The practice maintains an internal margin floor of 55% on every engagement. The floor is applied to net revenue after direct costs. Any engagement that would fall below that threshold is declined or restructured before it is accepted.

The floor is a financial discipline, not an approval process. It exists so that the practice can sustain research time, tools, and the quiet periods between engagements. The sole owner reviews it at the start of each calendar quarter.

Time estimates are indicative. The final fee depends on recorded hours and agreed direct costs. Clients receive a written estimate before work starts and a progress update whenever the estimate is likely to change materially.

## Discount authority

The practice has no partner and therefore no discount approval rule. The sole owner alone decides whether to vary fees for a particular client or engagement. There is no committee, no countersignature, and no second approval step.

As sole owner I may set any discount I consider appropriate for the circumstances. That discretion is exercised within the internal margin floor and is recorded in the engagement notes so that invoices remain accurate. A concession granted on one engagement does not apply to later work.

Discounts are expressed as a reduction on the stated hourly rate or as a fixed cap on total fees. They must be confirmed in writing before the relevant work starts. The sole owner may withdraw an unaccepted offer at any time.

## Confidentiality and records

Client information is treated as confidential. It is used only to deliver the engagement and is not shared except where the client has given written consent or where disclosure is required by law. Working files are retained for a reasonable period and then destroyed.

The sole owner keeps simple records of hours, invoices, direct costs, and any concession granted.

## Scope, changes, and termination

Either party may end an engagement with written notice. Work completed up to the notice date is invoiced at the agreed rate, together with any committed direct costs. These terms may be updated from time to time; the version in force at the start of an engagement applies to that engagement.

Nothing in these terms creates employment, a partnership, a joint venture, or an agency relationship. The sole owner remains responsible for all tax filings and statutory obligations of the practice.
