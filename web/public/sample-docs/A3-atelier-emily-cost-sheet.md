# Atelier Emily ("the Company") — Cost Sheet

**Document:** Atelier Emily Cost Sheet
**Version:** 1 October 2026
**Prepared for:** founders and finance review
**Scope:** current core collection

## Purpose

This cost sheet records the standard list prices, direct unit costs, and gross margins for the core garments currently sold by the Company. Figures are shown per unit and are reviewed at each operations meeting. The retail price is the published price before any discount, while COGS covers materials, cut-and-sew labour, finishing, and packing.

The flagship SKU Aurora Linen Dress remains the benchmark product for margin discussions. The other items follow the same pricing rules: retail price and COGS are confirmed against supplier invoices, and gross margin is calculated on the net selling price. Finance maintains the underlying invoices and reconciles them to this sheet before each review.

## Notes

The margin percentages shown above are calculated at full retail price, before any concession. They are not a licence to discount below the approved threshold. Finance will recompute the margin whenever a supplier invoice changes the direct unit cost, and will circulate an updated sheet if any figure moves outside the approved range.

The sales team must check the current sheet before quoting a wholesale order. Quoted prices must be consistent with the published retail price and with the approval rules recorded in the partnership agreement. A request that falls outside those rules must be escalated rather than quoted informally.

All prices subject to the margin floor of 55% and the 10% discount cap.
