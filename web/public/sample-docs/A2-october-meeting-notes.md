## Purpose

The team met to review the flagship product economics, confirm the pricing rules recorded in the partnership agreement, and agree on action items ahead of the holiday selling period. The meeting focused on the SKU Aurora Linen Dress and on the controls that apply to any proposed concession.

## 1. Recap of agreed product economics

The team confirmed the following figures for the flagship SKU Aurora Linen Dress:

- Retail price: $100 per unit.
- Cost of goods sold (COGS): $40 per unit.
- Resulting gross margin at full retail price: 60%.

These figures match the partnership agreement and the cost sheet circulated before the meeting. Finance will keep them as the baseline for all discount calculations. No change to the retail price or COGS was proposed.

## 2. Confirmed pricing controls

The attendees reconfirmed the two standing controls that govern sales decisions:

- The margin floor of 55% remains in force. No garment may be sold below that floor.
- The standard discount cap of 10% remains in force. A discount within the cap may be granted without co-founder approval, provided the margin floor is respected.

Any request above the cap continues to require prior written approval from both founders. The team noted that a large order does not by itself override the approval requirement.

## 3. Holiday discount proposal

A proposed 20% holiday discount was not approved.

The proposal was considered for a seasonal campaign but was rejected because it breaches the margin floor. No exception will be made for this campaign. The team agreed to explore non-price incentives instead, such as free gift wrapping and earlier delivery windows, which do not reduce the net unit price.

## 4. Open items and risks

The production lead flagged longer lead times for linen fabric from the current supplier. The team discussed whether a second supplier should be qualified before the next collection. Finance noted that any increase in COGS would reduce the headroom between the current gross margin and the margin floor, so supplier pricing must be reviewed before the next price review.

The team also discussed the bulk tier rule. The approval log should record the quantity, the concession, and the resulting margin for every order above the standard cap.

## 5. Decisions

- The retail price and COGS for the SKU Aurora Linen Dress remain unchanged.
- The margin floor and the discount cap remain unchanged and continue to apply to every garment.
- The proposed holiday discount is rejected; alternative non-price incentives will be prepared.
- A second linen supplier will be shortlisted and assessed on cost and lead time.
