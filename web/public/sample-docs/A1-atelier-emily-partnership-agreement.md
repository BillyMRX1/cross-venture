# Atelier Emily ("the Company") — Partnership Agreement

**Document type:** Partnership Agreement
**Venture:** Atelier Emily ('the Company')
**Effective date:** November 2026
**Founder:** Emily Hartono
**Co-founder:** Sari Wijaya
**Registered office:** Jalan Kemang Raya 18, Jakarta Selatan, Indonesia

## Recitals

This Partnership Agreement is made and entered into as of November 2026 by and between Emily Hartono, an individual resident in Jakarta, and Sari Wijaya, an individual resident in Bandung, who together carry on the business of designing, manufacturing, and selling contemporary womenswear under the trading name Atelier Emily ('the Company').

The parties acknowledge that Atelier Emily is co-owned by Emily Hartono and Sari Wijaya in equal shares. The parties further acknowledge that the business was conceived as a joint creative and commercial undertaking, and that each party has contributed capital, design work, supplier relationships, and operational labour to the venture since its inception.

The purpose of this Agreement is to record the terms on which the parties operate the Company, to define their respective rights and obligations, to establish a clear decision-making framework, and to protect the commercial interests of the Company and of each partner. The parties intend this Agreement to be the governing document for all matters not expressly covered by a separate written resolution signed by both founders.

This Agreement supersedes all prior discussions, letters of intent, and informal understandings between the parties relating to the Company. No variation of this Agreement is effective unless it is made in writing and signed by both founders.

## Definitions and Interpretation

§1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context requires otherwise, the following terms have the meanings set out below. "Agreement" means this Partnership Agreement, including any schedules and written amendments. "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in Jakarta. "Founder" means either Emily Hartono or Sari Wijaya, and "founders" means both of them together.

§1.2 "Gross margin" means the difference between the net selling price of a garment and its cost of goods sold, expressed as a percentage of the net selling price. "Cost of goods sold" or "COGS" means the direct landed cost of materials, cutting, sewing, finishing, packaging, and inbound freight attributable to a single unit.

§1.3 "Retail price" means the standard published selling price of a product before any discount, promotion, or negotiated concession. "Discount" means any reduction from the retail price, whether expressed as a percentage, a fixed amount, a bundle, a free item, or an equivalent commercial concession.

§1.4 Headings are inserted for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. References to a section are references to a section of this Agreement. References to a person include that person's successors and permitted assigns. Words in the singular include the plural and vice versa.

## Capital, Profit, and Losses

§2.1 Each founder holds an equal partnership interest in the Company. Capital contributions are recorded in the capital account maintained by the Company. Additional capital may be contributed only with the written consent of both founders.

§2.2 Profits and losses are shared equally between the founders after payment of operating expenses, supplier invoices, taxes, and reasonable reserves. Distributions may be made quarterly, provided that the Company retains sufficient working capital for the following two collection cycles.

§2.3 No founder may withdraw capital, make a loan to or from the Company, or pledge Company assets without the prior written consent of the other founder. Any loan between a founder and the Company must be documented in writing and recorded in the Company's books.

§2.4 The founders will maintain accurate books and records, including sales records, supplier invoices, inventory counts, and bank statements. Each founder may inspect the books at any reasonable time and may request a summary of the Company's financial position.

## Roles and Responsibilities

§3.1 Emily Hartono is responsible for creative direction, product development, collection planning, and brand partnerships. Sari Wijaya is responsible for production planning, supplier management, quality control, and logistics. Operational responsibilities may be reallocated by written agreement.

§3.2 Each founder will devote such time and attention to the Company as is reasonably necessary for the proper conduct of the business. Neither founder may engage in a competing apparel business during the term of this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other founder.

§3.3 The founders will meet at least once per month to review sales, inventory, supplier performance, and cash flow. When reviewing customer concessions, the founder responsible for the retail account will consult with partner Emily Hartono, and will also consult with partner Sari Wijaya where production capacity is affected, before any commitment is made. Decisions affecting the core identity of the brand, the annual collection calendar, or the Company's material commitments require the agreement of both founders.

## Pricing, Margins, and Discounts

§4.1 **Flagship product pricing.** The flagship SKU Aurora Linen Dress has a standard retail price of $100 per unit. This published figure applies to every ordinary retail and wholesale channel and is reviewed by the founders twice each year.

The cost of goods sold (COGS) for the SKU Aurora Linen Dress is $40 per unit. Finance reconciles this figure to supplier invoices before each review and records any change in the approval log. The parties will review the retail price and COGS for each product at least twice per year, and any change to a published retail price takes effect only after both founders have confirmed the change in writing.

§4.2 **Margin floor.** To protect the financial health of the Company, no garment may be sold at a gross margin below the margin floor of 55%. Any proposed transaction that would produce a gross margin below this floor is not approved, regardless of order size, customer relationship, or seasonal considerations.

The founders will recalculate the margin on every quoted order and will retain the calculation in the approval log. This check applies to wholesale orders, retail promotions, sample sales, and any other arrangement that reduces the amount actually received for a garment.

§4.3 **Standard discount cap.** In the ordinary course of business, discounts of up to 10% may be granted without co-founder approval. A founder may approve such a discount for a retail customer, wholesale buyer, or repeat client. The requesting founder must confirm that the resulting gross margin remains at or above the approved threshold before the concession is entered in the approval log.

§4.4 **Above-cap approval.** Any discount above the 10% cap requires prior written co-founder approval. The requesting founder must provide the customer name, the SKU, the proposed price, the order quantity, and the resulting gross margin before approval is considered. Approval given for one transaction does not create a precedent or a standing exception.

§4.5 **Bulk tier.** As a limited exception to the standard cap, orders of 60 units or more may exceed the discount cap only with both founders' written approval. The founders will assess bulk requests case by case, taking into account production capacity, payment terms, return risk, and the effect on the Company's margin floor.

## Approvals and Records

§5.1 Each approval under this Agreement must be recorded in the Company's approval log. The log must identify the date, the requesting founder, the customer, the SKU, the retail price, the proposed discount, the order quantity, and the calculated gross margin.

§5.2 An approval is valid only for the transaction described in the request. If the customer changes the quantity, payment terms, delivery schedule, or product mix, the requesting founder must obtain a fresh approval before confirming the revised order.

§5.3 The founders will review the approval log at each monthly meeting. Any transaction that appears to breach the margin floor or the discount cap must be reported promptly, and the founders will agree on a remediation plan.

## Banking, Expenses, and Taxes

§6.1 The Company will maintain a dedicated bank account. Both founders are authorised signatories. Transfers above an amount agreed by the founders require two approvals. The Company will not commingle partnership funds with the personal funds of either founder.

§6.2 Each founder may incur routine operating expenses in accordance with the approved budget. Expenses outside the budget require prior written approval. Receipts must be submitted within fourteen days of the expense being incurred.

§6.3 The Company is responsible for its own taxes, duties, and statutory filings. Each founder remains responsible for personal tax obligations arising from distributions.

## Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

§7.1 The founders will keep confidential all non-public information relating to the Company, including supplier terms, pricing models, customer lists, design files, and financial records. This obligation continues after a founder ceases to participate in the Company.

§7.2 All designs, patterns, artwork, brand assets, and product documentation created for the Company are the property of the Company. Each founder assigns to the Company all rights necessary to use, protect, and commercialise those materials.

## Departure, Dispute Resolution, and Amendment

§8.1 If a founder wishes to withdraw from the Company, that founder must give at least sixty days' written notice. The remaining founder has the right to purchase the departing founder's interest at a valuation agreed by the founders or, failing agreement, by an independent valuer appointed jointly.

§8.2 The founders will attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through direct discussion within fourteen days of written notice of the dispute. If the dispute remains unresolved, the founders will refer the matter to mediation in Jakarta before commencing any legal proceedings.
